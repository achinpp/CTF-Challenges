FSOCIETY PASSPHRASE
Recruitment Division
============================================================

A hidden recruitment channel believed to belong to an
underground collective has gone silent. One intercepted
transmission survived, along with fragments recovered from
the operator's workstation.

The message is encrypted. The key was never stored directly.

Three artifacts remain:

    manifesto.txt       a statement left on the workstation
    operator.log        the last local session's diagnostic log
    transmission.enc    the surviving wire payload

One investigator left a note in the evidence record:

    "The password isn't written anywhere. But whoever wrote
     this wanted the right person to be able to reconstruct it."

Recover the hidden recruitment message and submit its token.
Everything you need is in this archive. Work offline; no live
service, account, password cracking, or paid tooling is needed.

## 2. Operation Ghost Employee

**Category:** OSINT
**Difficulty:** Easy

### Description

The destination identified during **The Missing Destination** confirms fsociety's suspicions: the organization operating from that location is the main external partner behind a confidential Evil Corp project.
Although the partner's location is now known, the project's purpose, personnel, and internal codename remain hidden. Evil Corp has carefully separated the project from its public operations, leaving almost no direct connection between the company, its partner, and the employees involved.
Mr. Robot believes that one former Evil Corp employee holds the missing piece of the puzzle. This employee may have worked closely with the external partner and left behind information capable of exposing the project's identity.
Unfortunately, Evil Corp has systematically removed nearly every public reference linking former employees to the project in an attempt to erase its existence.
Your mission is to identify the correct former employee and determine the codename of the confidential project they were involved with.
Discovering the codename will allow fsociety to recognize related communications, personnel records, and internal documents during the next phase of the investigation.
Begin your investigation here:
**http://evilcorp-financial.duckdns.org/**

Submit the flag in the following format:
`RedCypher{Project_<Codename>}`

### Notes

* No exploitation or brute force is required.
* Everything needed to solve the challenge is publicly accessible.
* Carefully gather, verify, and correlate information before drawing conclusions.
* Clue: If the information seems to have disappeared, look for older versions of the pages preserved from the past.


## 3. The FAX Coordinator

Every successful operation begins with intelligence.

The investigation into the former Evil Corp employee has revealed the codename of the confidential project: **FAX**.

With the project now identified, fsociety begins searching Evil Corp's infrastructure for any records connected to that name. During the operation, the team infiltrates one of Evil Corp's external file servers and intercepts a collection of routine corporate documents.

At first glance, the recovered files appear harmless—weekly emails, internal directories, organizational charts, and archived administrative records. Nothing seems confidential enough to expose the company's operations.

Mr. Robot is not convinced.

The external partner discovered during **The Missing Destination** and the former employee identified during **Operation Ghost Employee** prove that FAX is more than an abandoned internal project. Intelligence suggests that the initiative is still active and is now being coordinated by someone currently working inside Evil Corp.

No single recovered document directly identifies this person. Instead, the files contain scattered fragments of information that must be combined to reconstruct a complete personnel profile.

Your mission is to examine every recovered artifact, identify the employee responsible for the FAX initiative, and reconstruct their identity.

The resulting profile will give fsociety a confirmed internal target and provide the personnel details needed to identify accounts, communications, and credentials connected to the project.

Remember, not every file is immediately accessible, not every document tells the whole story, and not every clue is where you expect it to be.

# Next Challenge: Shadow's Legacy

## Story

Not every piece of intelligence proves valuable immediately.

Among the information recovered during a previous fsociety operation, Mr. Robot believes there may be a lead that everyone else has overlooked.

At first glance, it appears insignificant. But if his instincts are correct, it could expose a phishing campaign operated by an unknown threat actor known only as **Shadow**.

Your task is to follow every lead, reconstruct the attacker's infrastructure, and determine exactly who was compromised during the campaign. Recover the captured credentials before the trail disappears and use the intelligence to support the next phase of Operation Five/Nine.

## Objective

Recover the username and password of the compromised **Chief Technology Officer (CTO)** of **Evil Corp Financial**.

### Notes
*Clue: The target you're looking for isn't a stranger—the intelligence uncovered in the previous challenge may already contain the key to identifying the Chief Technology Officer.
*Clue: Follow Shadow's trail—you'll find a password-protected ZIP; think of John when you need to unlock it. Sometimes John show things we never thought.

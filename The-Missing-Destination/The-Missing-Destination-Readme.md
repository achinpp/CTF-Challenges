## 1. Challenge: The Missing Destination

During the early stages of Operation Five/Nine, Mr. Robot dispatches an fsociety reconnaissance team to observe one of Evil Corp's suspected meeting locations. Intelligence indicates that the destination may belong to an external organization secretly working with Evil Corp.

The organization is believed to be the main financial partner behind a confidential Evil Corp project, but its identity and exact location remain unknown.

Before the scout can complete the mission, communication is abruptly lost. The only information recovered from the damaged transmission is a pair of encoded coordinate strings and a handful of surveillance photographs.

Intelligence suggests that the recovered coordinates do not point directly to the destination. Instead, they identify the location where the scout began the surveillance. From there, the scout followed the target on foot, documenting the surrounding area before the transmission ended.

The photographs contain subtle clues about the scout's route. By following the path visible in the photographs, investigators may be able to identify two roads along the route: **S Broadway** and **Rosecrans Ave**. These roads provide important landmarks for reconstructing the scout's movements and narrowing down the missing destination.

Your task is to reconstruct the missing intelligence. Decode the recovered coordinates, begin your investigation from the indicated location, and use the supplied photographs to trace the scout's route through **S Broadway** and **Rosecrans Ave**. Follow the path until you can determine the exact destination the scout was attempting to document.

Identifying this destination may reveal the organization assisting Evil Corp with one of its most closely guarded projects and provide fsociety with the first lead needed to uncover the operation behind it.
